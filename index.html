Wireframe Revision History

This changelog documents all decisions made, changes applied, and items backlogged during the wireframe review cycle. It draws from feedback in the Accelerate Wireframe 🧑‍💻 Teams chat, comments in the Accelerate Landing Page .docx, and the Accelerate_Landing_Page_Web_Dev_Handoff.docx handoff file. Maintained by Nathanael Conway. Last updated: May 8, 2026.

✓ v2 — Approved Changes Applied (May 8, 2026)
Naming — Remove "Hub"

Replaced all references to "Hub" with neutral alternatives. UKG sells a branded product called "Great Place To Work Hub" — using that word here creates confusion for UKG-referred customers.

Raised by: Natalie Lyda (April 16) · Approved by: Nathanael Conway

Copy — All module body text replaced with approved copy

Wireframe placeholder text replaced with final reviewed copy from the Accelerate Landing Page .docx. Reviewed by Janan Barance, Melissa Young, Karna Campopiano, and Nathanael Conway across March–April 2026.

UX — CSM touchpoints made visually prominent

Added CSM touchpoint component to 4 key modules: Align your leadership, Build your employee data file, Add custom survey content, Turn feedback into action. Also added CSM note to add-ons section.

Raised by: Melissa Young · Supported by: Janan Barance

Copy — Hero subtitle, Phase 3 blurb, Culture Audit note, review notes

Hero subtitle updated with Trust Index™ and Certification language. Phase 3 blurb: "real value" replaces "long-term value." Culture Audit note: "Optional — for list consideration." Review notes polished for web dev handoff.

Copy — "Great Place To Work model" secondary label

"Model overview" replaced with "Great Place To Work model" to match the official framework name. Claire's suggestion to rename to "Effect" was declined — the Model and the Effect are separate concepts.

⏳ Backlogged — Decisions Deferred or Pending

Melissa asked if modules need numbers or arrows for order. Deferred to Janan and Jonathan as content owners.

Raised by: Melissa Young (April 7) · Status: Awaiting CX decision

Add-ons baked into relevant modules

Melissa suggested surfacing add-on links within the appropriate module. Needs Janan or Jonathan to map add-on to module.

Raised by: Melissa Young (April 7) · Status: Awaiting mapping

Claire and Melissa suggested top progress indicator. Nathanael noted it would get messy with add-ons included. Deferred to v2+.

Raised by: Claire Murphy (April 12), Melissa Young (April 7)

Saving user state on a public website without auth is not feasible for MVP. Requires login infrastructure.

Raised by: Melissa Young (April 7) · Decision: Not feasible for MVP

Gail's idea (via Natalie): evolve into a resource customers return to throughout their journey. Nathanael leans toward AI tools long-term.

Raised by: Gail via Natalie Lyda (April 16) · Decision: Future consideration

Maven / AI integration

Parallel workstream. Process doc includes a KB article step to lead Maven to reference the landing page. Not in wireframe scope.

Source: Karna Campopiano's Digital Journey meeting · Status: Parallel workstream

Claire suggested replacing "Great Place To Work model" with "Effect." Nathanael corrected: the Model is the foundational framework; the Effect is a separate concept shared later. They are not interchangeable.

Raised by: Claire Murphy (April 12) · Corrected by: Nathanael Conway (April 13) · Decision: Keep "Model"
