Change log

What we changed and why

A plain-English log of every update to this page. Read top to bottom for the latest first. Built and maintained by Nathanael Conway. Last updated: May 9, 2026.

✓ v3.10 — Mobile checkbox + rail + tutorial polish, Phase 1 copy refresh (May 9, 2026)
The phase rail reads cleanly on phones now

On narrow screens the rail's short titles ("Before launch" / "Survey live" / "After close") were getting cut to three or four letters next to the percent, which made the row hard to read. On phone widths the rail now hides the title text entirely and shows the phase number ("01" / "02" / "03") next to the percent. The section title above the row already names what the row is for, so nothing's lost.

The module checkbox now draws a full dashed border on phones

On real iPhones the dashed border around an empty checkbox could drop on one or two sides because the line was thin enough that the screen rounded it inconsistently. The line is now slightly thicker on small screens so all four sides render every time, while the box stays the same visible size.

The module checkbox is easier to tap on phones

The tappable area around each checkbox now matches Apple's recommended size for touch targets (about a thumb-pad worth of space). The visible box still looks the same; only the invisible tap region around it got bigger so you don't have to aim as carefully.

The tutorial now shows what a checked module looks like

Step 6 of the first-time tour explains "tap this checkbox when you complete a module." It used to show an empty box while telling you that. Now while that step is active the example checkbox is filled in green so you can see what "complete" actually looks like. As soon as you move to the next step (or finish the tour) the example clears. Your real progress is never touched.

Phase 1 copy refresh: roster module + Victoria's video

The Phase 1 module about your employee data file now reads "Get your Employee Data File launch-ready" instead of "Build your Employee Data File." The inline walkthrough video panel beside Victoria's photo also picked up new copy — it now leads with "What good data looks like" instead of repeating the module title, so you don't read the same words twice in the same card.

Link previews now show a real image when this URL is shared

Pasting the page link into Microsoft Teams, iMessage, WhatsApp, Slack, or LinkedIn used to render a blank or generic preview. Now it pulls in the hero image with "Great Starts Here," a clean title ("Accelerate Onboarding — Great Starts Here"), and a one-line description of what the page is for. The share image was rendered from the same hero artwork on this page so the preview matches what the recipient sees when they click through.

A 13-second demo video for Claire to share at Launch Pad

There's now a short demo video that walks through the page in 13 seconds — the hero panel, a sample module checking off in green, and the full "100% complete · Great Starts Here" celebration with the GPTW box logo. Claire Murphy will share it at Launch Pad as a quick preview of what new customers see. The video is built from the actual artwork on this page (real hero, real module checkbox, real celebration animation) so what people see in the clip matches what they'll see when they land on the page. Five full loops are baked into the file so it auto-replays on its own across email, Teams, iMessage, and Slack — recipients don't need to tap play again.

✓ v3.9 — Last-task and milestone notes, tutorial spotlights the new path buttons (May 9, 2026)
A short note when one module is left

When you're down to your final module, the small "need a hand" note that lives near the next-incomplete card switches its words: it tells you one module is left and offers a button that jumps you straight to it. Same gentle placement as before, just the right message for the moment.

Two quiet milestone notes along the way

Two new milestone notes appear once each on the way through. The first lands when you cross the halfway mark of the modules that apply to you. The second lands when you've finished every "for all customers" module while a path is still on, so you know the universal work is done and only path-specific steps remain. Each note shows once, then doesn't return — no celebration spam, no points, no streaks.

No "need a hand" note when you've finished

If every module that applies to you is checked off, the "need a hand" note doesn't appear at all. It only shows when there's actually something to help with.

The tour now points at each path button on its own step

The first-time tour used to walk you past a "Choose your route" folder that no longer exists. Now there are two dedicated tour steps — one lands on "Add Global Certification Onboarding" and explains when to tap it, the next lands on "Add UKG onboarding" and does the same. Splitting them keeps each spotlight focused on one decision at a time. "Check things off as you go" moves further down the tour so the order tracks how you'd actually use the page.

"Certification" is capitalized everywhere it appears

Certification is a Great Place To Work product name, so it now reads with a capital C in every visible spot — the Global Certification path button, the Phase 2 blurb and outcome ("complete the Certification application"), and the research blurb on the Trust Index Score Inputs resource ("the research story behind the Certification"). No spots that already capitalized it have changed.

Stats now show their final numbers right away

The "You're in great company" stats used to count up from zero (180+, 22k+, 20M+). The count-up has been removed. The numbers render at their final values immediately and stay visible while the tour is open. Same numbers, no animation, no waiting.

All progress notes now appear in the same corner — and stay quiet once you're done

Every nudge — the "need a hand" idle note, the halfway note, the universal-modules-done note, and the one-module-left note — now appears in the bottom-right corner of the page. Earlier in v3.9 the help note still anchored above the next module while the milestones sat in the corner, which felt scattered. One location for every note keeps the page predictable. The "need a hand" note also now stays hidden once your active path is complete, so a user returning to a finished page never sees a help offer for work that no longer exists.

Every module shows what it costs — in time or in effort

Every module now displays a small chip telling you what to expect: a time estimate when one exists ("~10 min", "~20–30 min"), or a plain effort label when the module isn't time-bounded ("Quick read", "Reference", "Optional, when ready", "Placeholder" for the path-filler cards). Modules that previously had no chip at all have one now. Screen readers also adapt — time values are announced as "Estimated time", everything else as "Effort".

The tour now points at the checkbox itself

In step 6, "Check things off as you go," the spotlight now lands on the actual checkbox control on the first module instead of the whole card. The thing the user is being asked to tap is the thing the arrow points at.

"Take me there" never lands on a hidden module

When a path is turned off (Global or UKG), its modules are hidden from the page. The "Take me there" button now skips those hidden modules entirely. Before, in a rare edge case it could try to scroll to a path you'd turned off — you'd see nothing happen because the target was hidden. Now the button only ever lands on a module that's actually visible.

The "Take me there" button now flashes the module it lands on

When you tap "Take me there" on the one-module-left note, the page now scrolls to the remaining module and the card pulses with a brief red ring so you can see exactly which one to finish. Before, the scroll was so small (the popup was right above the only remaining card) that it felt like nothing happened.

Victoria's "Employee Data File" video flipped

In Phase 1, the inline walkthrough video for "What good data looks like" used to sit with the photo on the left and the title on the right. We've flipped it: the title and description now sit on the left, with Victoria's photo and play button on the right. The thin divider between the two halves moved with the layout so it still lands between them. On phones the card stacks the same way it always has, photo on top.

✓ v3.8 — Choose-your-path buttons, hidden when off, popup re-anchored (May 9, 2026)
Choose your path with two simple buttons

The Phase 1 "Choose your route" used to show a folder shell with a "For all customers" branch and a hidden "Some customers" branch you had to expand. We replaced it with two plain toggle buttons that sit right under the Phase 1 title card: "Add Global certification onboarding" and "Add UKG onboarding." Tap a button to turn that path on; tap again to turn it off. The buttons remember your choice between visits.

Modules you don't need stay hidden, not dimmed

When a path is off, the modules tied to that path don't show up at all in the phase — they're not faded or dashed, they're hidden. Turn the button on and they appear inline in the right phase. Turn it back off and they disappear again. That keeps the page short for customers who don't run a global certification or aren't supported through UKG.

Your percent only counts what actually applies to you

The phase percent and the overall percent now ignore any modules whose path is turned off. So if you don't add either path, finishing the "for all customers" modules takes you to 100%. Turn Global on and the math automatically adds those modules to the count. Turn it back off and they leave the count again. No more chasing checks for steps that don't apply.

The "need help" note now points at the next thing to do

If you sit on the page for half a minute without checking anything off, a small "need a hand" note used to slide in from the bottom-right corner. Now it lands in the small gap right above the next module you haven't completed and lines up with that module's right edge, so the note and the module it's about are visually paired. If there's no incomplete module visible (you're at the top, or you've finished everything in view), it falls back to the corner so it's still findable.

✓ v3.7 — Sleeker progress bar, Phase 3 video matches Phase 1, quiet links removed (May 9, 2026)
The progress bar at the top got smaller on every screen

The sticky progress bar used to take up about a third of the screen on desktop and almost half the screen on a phone. The page felt like it belonged to the bar, not to the work. Now the bar is a tight one-row strip with the title, your overall percent, and three short phase chips below a thin progress line. On a phone it sits at about 90 pixels, which is around 11% of the screen. On a tablet and desktop it sits at about 110 pixels, around 14% of the screen. The bar still sticks to the top when you scroll, still saves your progress, and still tells screen readers when the autosave fires. The page content is what fills the screen now, the way it should.

Victoria's video matches Janan's again

In v3.5.1 we flipped Victoria's Phase 3 video to a side-by-side card, but it stopped looking like a bookend to Janan's Phase 1 video. Phase 3 now uses the same full-width photo on top with the title and "Watch video" link in a band below, so the start and the end of the journey look like a matching pair. On phones both still stack the same way they always have.

The "Quiet secondary link" placeholder is gone

Every module card used to show a faint "Quiet secondary link" line under its main button. It was a placeholder from an earlier draft and never carried real content. It's removed everywhere on the page so the action area of each module reads cleanly with just the primary button and the resource pills.

A small fix to how the change log is checked

The verifier (the safety net that makes sure the page hasn't broken between versions) used to look for the v3.3.1 row in the table by the exact inline style on its label. That made the row brittle — a future style cleanup could silently break the check. The row now carries a small data tag and the verifier looks for that tag. Same proof, less fragile. No visible change for anyone reading the page.

✓ v3.6 — File-size diet (May 9, 2026)
The page now loads faster — same look, less code

Trimmed the page from 386KB to 359KB without changing a single pixel. Pulled repeated style fragments into shared classes (so the same look is described once instead of fifty times) and stripped internal-only build comments that don't help anyone reading the page. Every section, font, color, photo, and layout is identical to v3.5.1 — the file is just smaller and quicker to load.

✓ v3.5.1 — Phase 3 video flip, plain-English video copy, outcome card breathing room, screen-reader cleanup (May 9, 2026)
Victoria's video sits side-by-side now

In Phase 3, Victoria's photo and play button used to sit on top of the description. Now they sit next to each other on desktop — the words on the left, her photo on the right — so the section reads like a normal two-column layout. On phones it still stacks. Janan's video in Phase 1 wasn't touched.

Screen readers now hear the title first, not the eyebrow

Both video cards used to make screen readers say "A note from your team" before the actual video title and presenter's name. The eyebrow is decorative styling, not real content, so it's now hidden from screen readers. Sighted users see no change. Now screen readers hear the video title and presenter name first, the way the page actually reads.

The video strip sounds like a person now

The little strip under each video used to say "Watch 70% — or mark complete" with a "Mark complete" button. That's our internal language. Now it says "Watching counts as done — or check it off →" with an "I've watched it" checkbox. After you watch (or check), it says "Watched — counts toward this phase." The "Watching… (simulated)" placeholder also got cleaned up to "Playing video…".

The red "By the end of this phase" card has space to breathe

Inside each phase, the red-fading promise card was sitting flush against the description above it — they read as one block. Bumped the gap by 24 pixels so the promise card stands on its own.

✓ v3.5 — Phase 3 trim, scroll-jump fix (May 9, 2026)
Phase 3 is shorter now

Removed two modules from "After your survey closes": Culture and business insights and Join the community. They were optional/community items, not core to the first-week onboarding job. Phase 3 now has 2 modules + Victoria's video, and 100% completion no longer requires checking those two off.

Clicking a phase no longer hides the top of the section behind the bar

Before: clicking Before, Live, or After on the top progress bar scrolled the page, but the bar stayed pinned and overhung the start of the section, cutting off the first line. The fix reads the bar's actual rendered height in real time and adds a 24px buffer, so every jump (rail click, "Need a hand?", finish handoff, idle nudge, anchor link) lands flush below the bar regardless of viewport size or whether the bar is in mobile-collapsed or desktop-expanded state.

Names, links, and the bottom row are accurate

Janan's video subtitle now says "Janan Barance, Director, Customer Success" instead of "your CSM" — she's a director, not a personal CSM. The Upcoming Webinars card in the bottom resource row picked up the same UTM tracking the other cards have. The "5 well-being tips" article was pulled from that row so it doesn't get stale. The earlier change-log entry that listed Victoria as "VP" was corrected to her real title, "Sr. Customer Success Manager."

✓ v3.4 — Boxy look, better save, video credit, finish handoff, real resources (May 9, 2026)
The page is fully boxy now

Every card, button, and box on the page now has square corners, like the brand red box logo. The only round things left are the small dots that show status (a green dot, an autosave dot, the pulse on your current step). Those stay round on purpose — they look like buttons.

"Pick up where you left off" actually works

A banner used to say "pick up where you left off" but it was looking in the wrong place to find your progress, so it never showed up. Now it looks in the right place. If you check a box, leave the page, and come back — the banner will show and jump you back to the phase you were on.

Watching the videos counts as progress

Janan's video in Phase 1 and Victoria's video in Phase 3 now count toward finishing the phase. Click "Watch video" to play it — you get credit when you've watched most of the way. Or check the box yourself if you've already seen it.

Names are right now

Janan's title now reads "Director, Customer Success." Victoria's title reads "Sr. Customer Success Manager." Both pulled from the official team page so they match what we say everywhere else.

"Need a hand?" button now does something

The "Need a hand? Show me where" link in the welcome area used to do nothing. Now it scrolls down and gently highlights the first phase so you know exactly where to start.

Big finish — "Great Starts Here"

When you finish all three phases, the celebration animation now ends with the brand red box logo popping in and the words "Great Starts Here." A nice send-off so the moment lands.

Stats area looks tighter

The "180+ Countries / 22k+ Companies / 20M+ Employees" row used to feel a bit floaty. Spacing between the cards and around the title was pulled in so it reads as one tidy block.

When you finish, the page steps aside

When you check the last box, the sticky progress bar at the top tucks itself away. The page scrolls you down to the "Everything is in place" handoff. The little add-on call-outs inside the phases dim down so they aren't shouting at you anymore. The page is done. You're done. Now look around.

Better stuff to look at next

The bottom of the page used to be four "add-on" placeholder cards. Now it's the real things our customers actually use: the research that proves culture pays off, our free webinars, our podcast, the For All Summit, and our weekly Culture Edge newsletter on LinkedIn. Add-ons themselves now live up inside the phases where they fit best.

Easier to read for everyone

"You're in great company" stats
